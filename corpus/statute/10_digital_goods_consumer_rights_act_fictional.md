# Digital Goods Consumer Rights Act

**Version 1.0. Effective 1 January 2026.**

*Synthetic document for a learning project. This is an invented law written for a fictional platform. It is not real legislation.*

---

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This Act applies to a contract under which a seller supplies digital content or a digital service to a consumer.

**1.2** "Digital content" means data produced and supplied in digital form, including files, courses and downloads.

## 2. Quality

**2.1** Digital content must work as described and be fit for the purpose for which digital content of that kind is normally used.

**2.2** A seller may not refuse a remedy under this Act only because the consumer has already downloaded or accessed the digital content.

## 3. Remedies

**3.1** If digital content does not conform to the contract, the consumer is entitled to have it brought into conformity by repair or replacement.

**3.2** If conformity is not restored within a reasonable time, or the defect is serious, the consumer is entitled to a full refund.

**3.3** A refund under this Act is paid within 21 days of the seller agreeing that the consumer is entitled to it.

## 4. Terms that limit these rights

**4.1** A term in a contract that excludes or limits the consumer's rights under this Act is not binding on the consumer.

**4.2** A seller's own refund policy may give the consumer more than this Act provides, but never less.
