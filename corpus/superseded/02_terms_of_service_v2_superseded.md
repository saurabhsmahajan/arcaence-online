## 3. Subscriptions and billing

**3.1** Plans and prices are set out in Schedule A. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay for that Billing Cycle.

**3.2** Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each Billing Cycle unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

**3.3** If you were charged for a renewal you did not intend, you may request a refund within 14 days of the renewal charge, provided you have not used the Plan during the new Billing Cycle.

**3.4** Upgrades take effect immediately. We charge the price difference for the remainder of the current Billing Cycle, calculated under section 3.5.

**3.5** The price difference is calculated by dividing the difference between the old and new Plan price by 30, multiplying by the number of days remaining, and rounding to the nearest cent. This is called Proration.

**3.6** Downgrades take effect at the end of the current Billing Cycle. No refund is given for the remainder of the Billing Cycle.

**3.7** If a subscription payment fails, we try again up to three times over seven days. If all attempts fail, we suspend access to the Plan under section 6.2.

**3.8** We give at least 30 days' notice by email before changing the price of a Plan.
